## 🎨 Visual Identity (Swiss Style):
- **Stark High-Contrast Monochrome Grid:** Mathematical 1px grid lines and charcoal dark structure (`#0c0d0f`, `#14161a`).
- **Signature Swiss Accent:** International Safety Orange (`#ff4800`) for data highlights and alarms.
- **Grotesque Typography:** Strict typographic hierarchy using Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono.
- **Zero Fluff:** Total focus on analytical data density and laboratory precision.

---

## 🌟 Quality Engineering Capabilities:
- **Shewhart X-bar Control Charts:** Automated calculation of UCL, CL, and LCL with subgroup sample sizing.
- **Nelson 8-Rule Diagnostic Engine:** Real-time detection of process shifts, tool wear, and special cause variation.
- **Gaussian Capability Distribution:** Visualizing process spread against LSL, Nominal Target, and USL ($C_p, C_{pk}$).
- **Instant Sample Generator:** One-click simulation of Six Sigma benchmarks, process drifts, and excessive variances.
